Conan Gray is an American singer, songwriter and former Youtuber
“Nauseous” is one of my favorite songs from the album Wishbone because I really connect with it. It expresses the fear of loving someone and struggling to trust, which can sometimes be very hard. The album Wishbone explores intense heartbreak, queer relationship struggles, and deep self-reflection. I listen to these songs because my friends and I love connecting through music singing together, having fun, and creating meaningful memories. Those moments are really important to me.
